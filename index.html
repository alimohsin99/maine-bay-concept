01Our story

From Winter Harbor to Elmwood Street.

Shaun grew up in Winter Harbor, Maine, where the bay was part of everyday life. Years later, while teaching at Penn State, every trip home ended with a truck full of lobster, scallops and blueberries for friends who could not find anything like it in Central PA.

Christa kept saying it should be a business. In August 2017 it became one. Today Maine Bay & Berry partners with more than 15 small Maine businesses, so everything on our shelves comes from people we know and trust.

06Meet Shaun & Christa

A truck full of seafood, and an idea.

Shaun was born and raised in Winter Harbor, Maine, and taught management at Penn State's Smeal College of Business for 15 years. Christa earned her business degree at Penn State and worked in the Executive MBA program. They met at Smeal.

Whenever Shaun went home, he emailed about 130 friends offering to bring back lobster, scallops and blueberries. The truck always came back full. Christa kept telling him it should be a business, and one summer night on the drive home, they found their spot.

08From our kitchen

How to steam a Maine lobster.

The classic, no-fuss way. Ask us in store for tips on anything you take home.

  1. Bring about two inches of salted water to a rolling boil in a large pot with a steaming rack.
  2. Add the live lobsters head first, cover tightly, and start your timer.
  3. Steam roughly 12 to 15 minutes for a 1 to 1.25 lb lobster, a few minutes more for larger ones.
  4. Done when the shells are bright red and the meat is opaque white.
  5. Serve with melted butter and lemon. Corn on the cob is encouraged.
